About us

I'm Ray Ostler. I grew up in Hillyard and started plumbing in 1994 as an apprentice with a commercial outfit downtown. After nine years of hospitals and office buildings, I bought a used van and started doing houses on my own. That was 2003. My wife Jen kept the books at our kitchen table, and most of my first customers were people on our block.

We moved into the shop on Market Street in 2011. There are five of us now. We still only do houses, and most of them are older. We like old houses, and there are a lot of them in this town that need someone who won't panic when they open a wall.

We never had a website. For twenty years we didn't need one. People called because a neighbor or their realtor told them to, and that kept us plenty busy. But these days most people look for a plumber on their phone, and our customers kept telling us they had a hard time finding our number. So here we are.

The way we work hasn't changed. We tell you what's wrong, what it costs, and whether it really needs fixing today. Then we do the job right and clean up after ourselves.

— Ray
