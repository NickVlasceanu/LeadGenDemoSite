Water heaters

No hot water is one of the most common calls we get. Sometimes it's a part. Sometimes the tank is done. We'll tell you which, and we won't sell you a new one if yours still has years left in it.

Signs your water heater needs a look

  • Popping or rumbling when it heats. That's sediment cooking at the bottom of the tank. Our water here is on the hard side, so it builds up faster than people expect.
  • Hot water runs out quicker than it used to.
  • Rusty or brown water from the hot side only.
  • Water pooling around the base, or a drip from the relief valve pipe.
  • The tank is over 10 to 12 years old. The date is usually on the sticker. Call us with the serial number and we can read it for you.

What we do

First we figure out what's actually wrong. A lot of no-hot-water calls are a thermocouple, a gas valve, or a burned-out element, and those are a fraction of the cost of a new tank.

If the tank is leaking or rusted through, we replace it. We keep standard 40 and 50 gallon gas and electric tanks at the shop, so most replacements happen the same day or the next morning. The price covers the permit, bringing it up to current code (expansion tank, seismic straps, venting), and hauling the old one away.

We also install tankless heaters. They make sense for some houses and not others, and we'll be straight with you about which one yours is.

Questions we hear a lot

How long does a water heater last in Spokane?

For a regular tank, figure 8 to 12 years. Flushing it once a year and swapping the anode rod around year five can push it toward the long end.

Should I switch to tankless?

Maybe. You get endless hot water and it takes less space, but it costs about twice as much to put in, and the savings on your gas bill take a long time to pay that back. If you have a big family, or the tank keeps running out, it's worth talking about. If one tank has been fine for you, we'll usually tell you to stick with one.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater?

Yes. We pull it, and it's included in the price.

Can you come today?

If you call in the morning, usually yes. If not, it's normally the next morning.
