Leak repair

Some leaks are obvious. Others only show up as a water bill that jumped $60 for no reason. We find both, and we fix them without tearing up more of your house than we have to.

Signs of a leak

  • Your water bill went up and your habits didn't change.
  • A toilet that runs on its own, or refills every so often when nobody used it.
  • Stains or soft spots on a ceiling, or warped flooring near a tub or sink.
  • A musty smell under a sink or in a closet next to a bathroom.
  • A shutoff valve that drips, or won't turn off all the way when you need it to.

Check it yourself first

Turn off every faucet and make sure nothing is running, then go look at your water meter. Most of them have a small triangle or dial that spins when water is moving. If it's spinning, you have a leak somewhere. Write down the reading, wait two hours without using any water, and check again. Tell us what you find when you call. It helps.

What we do

We fix faucets, toilets, supply lines, shutoff valves and leaking pipe in walls, ceilings and crawlspaces. For hidden leaks we use moisture meters and listening gear to narrow it down before we open anything. If we have to cut drywall, we cut the smallest hole we can. We don't do drywall repair ourselves, but we can give you the name of the person we send everyone to.

Questions we hear a lot

Where is my main water shutoff?

In most Spokane houses it's in the basement or crawlspace, on the wall facing the street, where the water line comes in. There's also one at the meter by the curb, but you usually need a meter key for that. If you don't know where yours is, ask us next time we're out. We'll show you and hang a tag on it.

Is a running toilet really a big deal?

It can waste a couple hundred gallons a day without making much noise. Usually the fix is a flapper or fill valve, which is cheap. Worth doing.

Will insurance cover my leak?

It depends on your policy and how long the leak has been going. Sudden leaks are often covered and slow ones often aren't. We'll take photos and write up what we found so you have it for the claim.
