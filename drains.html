Drain cleaning

A slow kitchen sink is a nuisance. Water coming up through the basement floor drain is a different kind of day. We handle both, and we'll tell you which one you're dealing with.

Is it one drain or the main line?

If only one sink or tub is slow, the clog is probably close to that fixture. It's usually grease, hair or soap, and it's a quick fix.

If several things act up at once, it's probably the main line. Look for toilets gurgling when the washer drains, water backing up into the tub, or the basement floor drain getting wet. In older neighborhoods like the South Hill, Manito and Audubon, it's usually tree roots getting into old clay or cast iron sewer pipe.

What we do

We clear the clog with a cable machine sized for the pipe. For main line clogs we'll usually run a camera afterward so you can see what caused it. If it's roots, we'll show you on the screen and give you a price to fix it properly. Whether you do that now, later or never is up to you. A lot of people keep the line cleared every year or two, and that's a fair choice too.

If we clear a drain and the same clog comes back within 30 days, we come back and clear it again for free.

Questions we hear a lot

Should I try drain cleaner from the store first?

We'd skip it. It's hard on older pipes, it rarely clears a real clog, and then the pipe is full of chemicals when we have to open it up. A plunger is fine. For a tub, pulling the hair out of the stopper fixes it more often than you'd think.

Why does my main line keep backing up every year or so?

Almost always roots. Clearing them buys you time, but they grow back through the same cracked joint. A camera will show where it is, and a spot repair at that joint usually ends it for good.

Do you use hydro jetting?

Sometimes, for grease buildup in kitchen lines or heavy scale. For most clogs a cable does the job and costs less, so that's where we start.
